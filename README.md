# Hotel Karnataka Suites — Demo Website

A single-page demo website (`index.html`, self-contained, no build step) built for **Hotel Karnataka Suites**, a business hotel in Peenya, Bengaluru, as part of Arya's website + AI video agency work.

- The rooms, tariffs and photos shown on the page are **sample placeholders**, clearly labelled on the page itself — they are to be replaced with the hotel's actual rooms, rates and photos once the owner confirms them.
- The rating shown (4.9★ from 59 Justdial ratings; Google ~4.8★) is from the hotel's public listings, researched Oct 2026.
- The page is built around direct booking: a "Book Direct & Save" banner and WhatsApp enquiry buttons, so guests can book with the hotel directly instead of through an OTA that charges commission.
- Photos are royalty-free stock images, credited in the page footer.

**No one at the hotel has been contacted about this demo yet.**
