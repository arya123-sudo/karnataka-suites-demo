Smart Travellers Know

Why Book Direct?

Booking apps sit between you and the hotel — and charge for it. Booking direct is better for you and for the hotel.

💰

Best Rate, No Markup

OTAs take 15–25% commission on every booking. When you book direct, there's no middleman — so the hotel can give you its best possible rate.

💬

Instant Confirmation

Message the hotel directly on WhatsApp and get your room confirmed in minutes — no call centres, no waiting on an app.

🕐

Flexible Requests

Early check-in, a quiet floor, an extra bed — talk to the hotel itself and get real answers, not app policies.

On a ₹2,500 room, up to ₹625 goes to the booking app as commission.

Book direct and that money stays with your stay — better rates for you, fair earnings for a 4.9★-rated local hotel. That's why this website exists.
